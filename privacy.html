ASN-MCP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-14

1. What this covers

ASN-MCP ("the App") is a private integration used internally by All Saints ("the Organization") to connect its QuickBooks Online company to internal reporting and reconciliation tools, including AI-assisted analysis via Claude (Anthropic). This policy describes what data the App accesses and how it is used.

2. Data accessed

Through the QuickBooks Online API, the App can read:

  • Financial reports (balance sheet, profit & loss, cash flow, trial balance, general ledger)
  • Transactions (invoices, bills, payments, deposits, journal entries)
  • Customer and vendor records, which may include donor names, emails, and balances
  • Chart of accounts, classes, and bank/credit card balances

With write access explicitly enabled by the Organization, the App can also create bank deposit records (for example, reconciling Stripe donation payouts against QuickBooks) after an explicit confirmation step.

3. How data is used

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is used solely to answer questions, generate reports, and reconcile transactions on behalf of the Organization, through conversations with an AI assistant operated by the Organization's own authorized staff. Data is not used for any purpose unrelated to the Organization's own internal financial administration.

4. Data storage & sharing

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally on Organization-controlled devices/servers, not in any third-party database operated by the App's developer. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except as necessary to:

  • Communicate with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API (the source of the data)
  • Communicate with Anthropic's Claude API, to process the Organization's own requests about its own data

5. Data retention

The App does not maintain its own persistent database of QuickBooks data. Data is retrieved on demand and used only for the duration of the request. OAuth tokens are retained locally until revoked by the Organization or expired by Intuit.

6. Your rights

The Organization can revoke the App's access to QuickBooks Online at any time from its Intuit account's connected apps settings.
